## 29. Community follow-up

### 29.1 Boss overtime and Raid revival

- The maintainer requested the extra Final Assault / Hidden Core overtime grace only for teams with an AI teammate.
  At fight start, a non-departed AI seat preserves the configured 150 real seconds; otherwise the threshold is the
  visible level countdown (120 real seconds). The decision stays fixed during that fight. The first deduction is at
  the following whole real second, at one team LP per real second. `overtimeAt` uses that same threshold.
- Ordinary Raid polls keep the 0.2.3 order: own field first, then remaining route distance and stable id, and they
  still jump only when the skill is ready or the member has been idle long enough and the range is empty.
  The maintainer confirmed one reselect immediately after a revive. That reselect alone orders by taunt, then
  remaining route distance, then id, and it jumps while the operator is not blocking, even if another enemy is
  already in range. Standing on an enemy's tile is blocking: the 0.25 s poll runs before that tick's block pass,
  so the reselect refreshes contact before it decides to stay. A retreat, a move, a redeploy that waited out its
  timer, the initial deployment and a Raid jump do not arm it. [ASSUMED] A revive is recognised as a knock-out
  redeploy that returns before `respawnAt`; the bond text does not name that timer split.
- Regression: `boss-ai-grace.test.js` covers both final fights with/without AI; `community-round29.test.js` covers
  Texas S2 with local enemies, remote taunt/route priority, blocked revival and no repeated self-trigger.

### 29.9 Ember proposal (#465) — current behavior preserved

The history in §22.8 and the 0.1.2 changelog records the chosen compromise for queued 深池逐火 enemies. Comparing
the current code with the proposed phase-order swap reproduces that risk: after a blocker takes the next warrior,
the three husk types can be finished by ranged hits in the current reveal window; the proposal hides them throughout

absolute invulnerability, but it removes the previously chosen counterplay window. The proposal is not adopted;
the existing phase-order assumption remains pending a better official comparison.

**The bond.** 巴别塔 `babelShip` — the ninth core bond (`isCore`, `bondType SEASON`, `powerIdList ['babel']`, weight

- 一档 (3 distinct members on board): every member's ATK **and** maxHP ×`(1 + 0.12 + 0.008·L)` — both attributes,
  which is why its base is below 炎 (ATK only) and 阿戈尔 (HP only). Rider: for every OTHER bond kind its members
  carry, all members gain ATK +2%, capped at +10%; only bond kinds available in the match count (a bond in
  `mode.inactiveBondIds` is skipped — OPEN-1, the maintainer's decision).
- 二档 (6 distinct members): at battle start the highest-ATK member becomes 【指挥】; allies on its 8 surrounding
  tiles deal damage ×`min(1.10 + 0.0007·L, 1.80)` and ignore `min(0.20 + 0.0004·L, 0.60)` of DEF and RES. The
  penetration shares 精准 tier 2's `defIgnorePct` / `resIgnorePct` mods and takes the strongest, never the sum

  逻各斯 T5, 维什戴尔 T6) and the official chess of 华法琳 T4 (奇迹), 魔王 T4 and 伊内丝 T4 (远见 / 突袭), which

**The injection channel.** `tools/content/custom.mjs` is the one place the build deviates from the official tables;

`modeBonds` is the build-level overlay: 巴别塔 joins `activeBondIds` of every mode, so it participates in the

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `act1autochess_gar_event_takedamage` | `damaged` (the holder) | HP the holder **actually lost** | 奥达 `garrison_163_a/_b` | `check_hp` **800** | 巴别塔 +1 / +2 | 8 / 16 |

- **The thresholds are measured, not guessed.** One standard battle let 奥达 bank 5823 damage, which at the original

  first landing) and 阿斯卡纶's 前 3 次击倒 pair (`garrison_50`'s official shape). No orphan row is left in
  `data/garrisons.json` and the old `bb.count` / `bb.check_cnt` keys are gone — `test/content/babel_traits.test.js`
  asserts both.
- **`_dealdamage` reads `damaged`, not `hit`** — the design asks for the damage ACTUALLY dealt, and `damaged` is the
  one engine event that carries it (the same number `damage.js applyHpLoss` adds to the attacker's `stats.dmg`; `hit`
  fires before mitigation, dodge and the 伤判 effects). Its three readings are `[ASSUMED]` and chosen to match
  `stats.dmg` rather than to invent a second definition: **(1)** only damage the holder dealt to an **enemy** — self
  damage and friendly fire are skipped exactly as `stats.dmg` skips them (`source.side !== target.side`), and an
  element gauge chip (`type === 'element'`) is not HP damage; **(2)** over-kill is **not** counted — the instance is
  clamped to `max(0, min(amount, target.hp))`, the HP the bar actually lost (the `_takedamage` axis could not do this:
  a lethal hit leaves the holder at 0 and the hook exposes no clamped value); **(3)** a 流失 (`battle.loseHp`) is
  excluded, symmetric with the 承伤 axis (it removes HP but is no 造成伤害 and never reaches `stats.dmg`). A threshold
  crossing is consumed whether or not it lands, as in `_takedamage`, and the same `busy` guard drops a `damaged` the
  layer gain itself caused.

  fields (`Battle.js`: `kind === 'normal'` only; `match/unitePhase.js`, `match/bossRounds.js`,

  巴别塔's per-round layer income is the two 战斗外 sources alone — 可露希尔 `<获得时>` +4 / +8 per acquisition and
  伊内丝 `<休整期结束时>` +5 / +10 per round. The maintainer accepted the thinner floor after the mode sweep

### 29.20 attrByBond: 部署时判定 (official alignment; maintainer, 2026-10-12)

The maintainer's ruling: the 作战能力 row whose official card carries the small print 「（※仅在每次部署时判定盟约层数）」
means exactly what it says — the **per-layer half is read once, at each (re)deployment**, and never again while the unit
stands on the field. The engine was stronger than that: it recomputed the row on **every layer gain**. Landed here, with
维什戴尔's 作战能力 **kept as one row** (`base_*` fixed half + per-step half, no number moved; research 15 §2 / §7
OPEN-10).

**The call chain that changed.**

- **Before.** `installReaders()` (`server/sim/content/garrisons/battle.js:634`) collected all four layer readers of
  `READERS` (`:536`) into one list, ran `recompute()` **at install** (i.e. before any deployment, in the `Battle`
  constructor — `server/sim/Battle.js:175` → `content/index.js installContent`), and registered ONE
  `battle.on('layerGain', …)` hook (`:646`) that scheduled `battle.after(0, recompute)`. `applyReader()` (`:603`) then
  re-read `k = stepsOf()` (`:542`) and re-applied the passive buff `gar:<gid>` — so every 特质 gain / bond proc that
  raised a layer moved a standing unit's ATK / HP (and 144's redeploy time, 115's running window, 101's hit scale).
- **After.** `installReaders()` splits the readers: `DEPLOY_READERS` (`:535`, today just
  `act1autochess_gar_eff_attrByBond`) is judged by the new `judgeAttrByBond()` (`:594`) from a `deploy` hook
  (`:656`), with **no** `layerGain` listener and **no** install-time application; the remaining three readers keep the
  old `layerGain` recompute (`:646`). `judgeAttrByBond()` computes `k = stepsOf()`, stores `it.k` and replaces
  `gar:<gid>` with `attrMods(bb, k)` — the same `passiveBuff` (`persist`, `allowDead`, `refresh: 'replace'`), so it is
  idempotent and re-entrancy-free.
- **Which deployments count.** Every `deploy` event of the unit: the battle-start deployment
  (`battle/lifecycle.js:35`), the 突袭 landing and every later redeploy (`battle/deploy.js:70`), the summon spawn path
  (`battle/summons.js:113`) and 【移动】 (`battle/tiles.js:56`, officially 「本质上为一次特殊的撤退-再部署行为」 and
  「可以通过移动行为多次触发部署时触发的效果」) — the elite `_b` ids are the same code path. A unit that never
  deploys gets nothing (before, it carried the buff from install on).
- **Scope: `attrByBond` only.** The sibling readers carry **no** per-deployment note in their official text and keep
  their timing: `respawnTimeByBond` 144 (read when the knock-out computes `respawnAt`), `attrByBond_add_onstart` 115
